## Prompt (à coller tel quel)

Ultra-realistic handheld smartphone footage filmed through the windshield of a car driving on the seafront boulevard of Libreville, Gabon, hot humid afternoon. Ahead in the left lane, a small white cargo truck with a beige canvas tarp reading "VOTRE ENTREPRISE" is tipping over to the right, wobbling dangerously on its right wheels, left wheels in the air, about to crash. A modern matte black box truck enters from the right side of the frame, accelerates, pulls up alongside in the right lane and gently leans its side against the tilting truck, pushing it back upright. The white truck drops back onto all four wheels with a small suspension bounce, then both trucks drive side by side, steady, away from the camera. The rear doors of the black truck show the NEAM logo exactly as in the last frame, unchanged. Real physics, real camera shake, natural sunlight and shadows, palm trees and the Atlantic ocean on the left, apartment buildings on the right, light traffic. No text overlays, no other logos, no billboards.

Négatif (si le modèle l'accepte) : CGI, cartoon, distorted logo, extra text, billboards, morphing trucks, melting wheels.
